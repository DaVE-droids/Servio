Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026

Servio is a field placement management platform used by students, lecturers, and placement coordinators to run internships (such as SIWES placements). It is operated by [Operator name] (“we”). This policy explains what information Servio collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how to delete it.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Your name, email address, department, faculty, and matric number or staff IDTo create your account and show who you are to the people supervising or coordinating your placement
Your phone number (optional)So your lecturer or coordinator can contact you
A profile photo (optional)Shown next to your name
Placement details: company name and address (with its map location), your company supervisor’s name and phone number, and your placement datesSo a coordinator can approve the placement and your lecturer can find and visit it
Attendance: the date and time you check in and outTo record the days you attended your placement
Logbook entries: what you worked on, learned, the challenges you met, and hours workedYour digital logbook, reviewed by your lecturer
Supervision visit reports written by lecturersTo record supervision visits
A record of key actions (for example, “placement approved”), with who did them and whenAn audit trail, so the institution can account for every decision

Servio does not track your location in the background, does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your information.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile, placement, attendance, logbook, and notifications.
  • Your assigned lecturer can see your profile, placement, attendance, and logbook, and writes your visit reports.
  • Placement coordinators at your institution can see all students, lecturers, placements, and the audit trail.

These permissions are enforced by the database’s security rules, not only by the app.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC) provides sign-in, the database, file storage, and website hosting. Servio’s data is stored in Google Cloud’s europe-west3 (Frankfurt, Germany) region. Firebase privacy information.
  • LocationIQ (Unwired Labs) suggests addresses as you type your company’s address. Only the text you type in that field is sent to it. LocationIQ privacy policy.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile are kept until you delete your account.
  • Placement, attendance, logbook, and supervision records form part of your institution’s academic records and are kept according to the institution’s record-keeping policies, including after an account is deleted.
  • Audit records cannot be edited or deleted, so that the history of decisions stays reliable.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile & settings (your picture, top right) and choose Delete my account. This permanently deletes your sign-in, your profile, and your profile photo. Records described above as academic records are kept by the institution. If you can’t sign in, email [contact email] to ask for deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to how it is used. You can correct most of your details in the app yourself. For anything else, contact us at [contact email].

Security

All connections to Servio are encrypted (HTTPS). Access to data is restricted by role, and sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication.

Children

Servio is intended for students in higher education and for staff. It is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell users in the app.
